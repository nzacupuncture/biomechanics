Moment arms in cm. Positive means the pull drives elevation, horizontal adduction, external rotation or elbow flexion; negative opposes it. Horizontal values appear once the arm is above 15°. Each muscle is one centroid line; cuff and deltoid wrap over the humeral head, triceps over the back of the elbow. The scapula does not move, so the sliders are glenohumeral angles: real 90° arm elevation is only about 60° at this joint. Swimmer view lays the body prone in the water: body roll changes where the arm is relative to the water, not any muscle length, because every muscle here rolls with the trunk.
