MuscleLengthElevHorizRot

Moment arms in cm. Positive means the pull drives elevation, horizontal adduction or external rotation; negative opposes it. Horizontal values appear once the arm is above 15°. Each muscle is one centroid line; cuff and deltoid wrap over the humeral head. The scapula does not move, so the sliders are glenohumeral angles: real 90° arm elevation is only about 60° at this joint.
