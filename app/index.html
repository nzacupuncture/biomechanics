Key: each column names two opposite movements: a positive value (green) means the muscle drives the + movement, a negative value (red) the − movement. Abd / Add = abduction / adduction; Flex / Ext = flexion / extension; H-add / H-abd = horizontal adduction / abduction; ER / IR = external / internal rotation; Up / Down = upward / downward rotation of the scapula; Post / Ant = posterior / anterior tilt; Off / On = medial border lifted off / held on the ribs; Elev / Depr = elevation / depression; Retr / Prot = retraction / protraction.

Moment arms in cm; positive means the pull drives the first movement named. Shoulder moment arms are about the glenohumeral joint; horizontal values appear above 15°. With the scapula moving with the arm, the shoulder sliders are the arm relative to the trunk and the scapula and clavicle follow published healthy rhythm (Ludewig et al. 2009): about a third of the elevation happens at the scapula. Choose Fixed to see the joint on its own, or Set by hand to explore dyskinesis and winging. Scapular muscles are single lines (serratus wraps round the chest wall); their upward-rotation values are about the whole girdle and are least reliable at high elevation. Swimmer view lays the body prone: roll changes where the arm is in the water, not muscle lengths.
